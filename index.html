In blocks. Cities that can't be reached in a full batch within this are left unbatched (small faint diamonds on the map); right-click one to add it to a batch. 0 means no limit.

    How to use

    1. Pick a batch

    Batches are in the list on the left, numbered outward from 0,0. Click one, click any city on the map, or type your position above the map and press Nearest batch. Can't see the list? Fold away the or scroll that panel.

    2. Get the waypoints into your map mod

    With a batch open, choose Xaero's Minimap to download a waypoint file, or JourneyMap / Other to copy chat lines that become clickable waypoints.

    3. Tick cities off as you loot them

    Use the checkboxes in the batch, or right-click a city on the map. Looted cities turn into an × and are left out of exports.

    4. Share what you looted

    Your ticks stay in this browser. To mark them for everyone, open on the left and send them in. Once accepted onto the shared list, those cities show as looted for all players.

    Good to know

    • Small faint diamonds are cities with a ship that didn't fit a batch. Right-click one to add it to the batch you have open.
    • Want your own route? Right-click any city and choose Start a custom batch with this city, then right-click others to add them. Custom batches sit at the top of the batch list.
    • Green shading is terrain already on the community webmap; cities there are left out of batches.
    • on the left changes the distance, angle, batch size and longest flight.
    • Drag the dividers beside the map to resize the panels.
