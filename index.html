    How to use

    1. Pick a route

    Routes are in the , numbered outward from 0,0. Pick one there, select any city on the map, or type your position into the x and z boxes on the map (or right-click the map and choose Set my position here) and press Nearest route.

    2. Get the waypoints into your map mod

    With a route open, choose Xaero's Minimap to copy waypoints to paste into its waypoint file, or JourneyMap / Other to copy chat lines that become clickable waypoints. Xaero's Minimap can take chat lines too.

    3. Tick cities off as you loot them

    Use the checkboxes in the route, or right-click a city on the map (press and hold on a touch screen). Looted cities turn into an × and are left out of exports.

    4. Share where you have been

    The best way is to upload your map of the End to the community webmap. By default, areas on the webmap are left out of the routes once this app's webmap data is refreshed.

    Your looted ticks stay in this browser. You can also send them in from ; once accepted, those cities show as looted for all players.

    Good to know

    • For Minecraft Java Edition, not Bedrock. It follows the 1.19–1.21 world generation rules and is in use on a 26.2 server; a future version that changes End generation could put cities elsewhere.
    • A ? after a city in a route means its ship is uncertain: it is an unusually tight fit in that city, and one ship like that turned out not to exist. Most may be fine. If you visit one, right-click it and report whether the ship was there.
    • Found a city with no ship, or no city where the app shows one? Right-click it, choose Report missing structure and pick which. Once accepted, it shows as a grey × for everyone and is left out of routes; hover over it to see what was reported.
    • Small faint diamonds are cities with a ship that didn't fit a route. Right-click one to add it to the route you have open.
    • Far from 0,0? In , choose Near my coordinates to search a circle around your coordinates.
    • Want to plan your own? Right-click any city and choose Start a custom route with this city, then right-click others to add them. Custom routes sit at the top of the route list.
    • Green shading is terrain already on the community webmap; by default, cities there are left out of routes.
