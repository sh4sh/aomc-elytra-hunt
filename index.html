    How to use

    1. Pick a route

    Route 1 takes the least flying. Pick one from the , click a ship on the map, or open Search near me and type your x and z.

    2. Get the waypoints into your map mod

    Choose your map mod and enter your username. Then, in the End, go down the route's list: press copy beside a ship, paste into chat, send, and add the waypoint that appears.

    3. Loot

    Press Next ship as you go. If the elytra was already gone, press Looted by someone else instead.

    4. Share where you have been

    Upload your map of the End to the community webmap, or send your looted list from .

    Changing a route
    • Add +1 ship to route, under the list, adds the nearest ship that has no route.
    • Right-click a ship, or press ⋯ on its row, to add it to the open route or remove it.
    • End route here stops a route part-way: the ships you looted are put away, and the rest are grouped into new routes.
    • Drag a ship up or down the list to change the order.
    • Tap ⋯ on a row and choose Move up or Move down to change the order.
    • The arrows under Next ship undo and redo your changes.
    • For a route of your own, right-click a ship and choose Start a custom route with this ship.
    Possibly looted ships
    • A ship marked looted by someone else shows as a tan cross in a box.
    • Ships within 2,000 blocks of it, or within 10,000 blocks of End Spawn, show as possibly looted: a grey ring, and a ? in the list. It is a guess; a route's why? link gives the reason.
    • A dashed line is a guess at where that hunter flew.
    • keeps them out of the routes.
    Something not as shown?
    • No ship where the app shows one, or no End City at all? Right-click it and choose Report incorrect….
    • The app knows where ships generate, not whether the elytra has been taken.
    Good to know
    • For Minecraft Java Edition, not Bedrock.
    • To keep track in game, delete a ship's waypoint in your map mod once you have looted it.
    • Map: drag to pan, scroll or use the slider to zoom.
    • Map: two fingers to pan, pinch or use the slider to zoom.
    • Faint diamonds are ships that are not in any route.
    • Green shading is terrain already on the community webmap; ships there are left out.
    • Far from End Spawn? Open Search near me and type your x and z. sets how far around you it looks.
    • Drag the dividers either side of the map to resize the panels.
