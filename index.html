    How to use

    1. Pick a route

    Press Find cities. Route 1 takes the least flying. Pick one from the , click a city on the map, or type your x and z and press Search near me.

    2. Get the waypoints into your map mod

    Choose your map mod and enter your username. Then, in the End, go down the route's list: press copy beside a city, paste into chat, send, and add the waypoint that appears.

    3. Loot

    Press Next city as you go. If the elytra was already gone, press Looted by someone else instead.

    4. Share where you have been

    Upload your map of the End to the community webmap, or send your looted list from .

    Changing a route
    • Add +1 city to route, under the list, adds the nearest city that has no route.
    • Right-click a city to add it to the open route, or remove it. On a touch screen, tap ⋯ on its row, or options under the map.
    • Drag a city up or down the list to change the order.
    • Tap ⋯ on a row and choose Move up or Move down to change the order.
    • The arrows under Next city undo and redo your changes.
    • For a route of your own, right-click a city and choose Start a custom route with this city.
    Possibly looted cities
    • A city marked looted by someone else turns tan. Someone hunted there before you.
    • Cities within 2,000 blocks of it show as possibly looted: a small grey ring, and a ? in the list. It is a guess.
    • So do all cities within 10,000 blocks of End Spawn: most ships there were emptied long ago. A route with such cities has a why? link explaining each reason.
    • When three or more such cities line up, a dashed line guesses where that hunter flew, and says how confident it is. One or two cities on the webmap can help to complete a line.
    • keeps them out of the routes.
    Something not as shown?
    • No ship, or no city, where the app shows one? Right-click it and choose Report incorrect….
    • The app knows where ships generate, not whether the elytra has been taken.
    Good to know
    • For Minecraft Java Edition, not Bedrock.
    • Map: drag to pan, scroll or use the slider to zoom.
    • Map: two fingers to pan, pinch or use the slider to zoom.
    • Faint diamonds are cities with a ship that are not in any route.
    • Green shading is terrain already on the community webmap; cities there are left out.
    • Far from 0,0? Type your x and z beside Search near me. sets how far around you it looks.
    • Drag the dividers either side of the map to resize the panels.
